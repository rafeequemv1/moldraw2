Aspirin, also called acetylsalicylic acid, is a salicylate NSAID and antiplatelet agent. This page gives the aspirin structure, identifiers, properties, class, and a direct MolDraw editor link.

Structure

Aspirin contains a benzene ring bearing a carboxylic acid and an acetylated phenolic oxygen. It is a common classroom example for ester functional groups and aromatic carboxylic acids.
