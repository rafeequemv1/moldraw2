FAQ

What is the formula of glucose?

Glucose has the molecular formula C6H12O6.

What is glucose's molecular weight?

The molecular weight is 180.16 g/mol and the exact mass is 180.06338810 Da.

Which functional groups are in glucose?

Glucose in cyclic glucopyranose form contains multiple hydroxyl groups, a cyclic hemiacetal, and an ether oxygen in the pyranose ring.
