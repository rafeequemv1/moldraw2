What is ChemSketch?

ChemSketch (also searched as ACD ChemSketch or ACD/ChemSketch) refers to chemical structure drawing software long associated with ACD/Labs. People use ChemSketch software to draw 2D structures, communicate molecules in reports, and support teaching or lab documentation.

Search demand for ChemSketch often mixes product discovery (“what is ChemSketch”) with practical need: open a chemical structure editor quickly, preferably free and online.

MolDraw is not ChemSketch and is not affiliated with ACD/Labs. It is an independent free browser app from Scidart Academy that covers many of the same day-to-day drawing intents.
