# MolDraw — Chemical Draw Online

MolDraw is a free browser-based chemical structure editor and 3D molecular viewer published by Scidart Academy.

## What you can do

- Draw and edit 2D chemical structures in the browser
- View molecules in 3D
- Search molecules by name (PubChem-backed workflows)
- Export PNG, SVG, SDF, XYZ, PDB, and other chemistry formats
- Use free converters and calculators at `/tools/free-chem-tools/`

## Disambiguation

MolDraw at moldraw.com is an independent product and is not ChemDraw (PerkinElmer). It is often used as a free online alternative for drawing and export workflows.
