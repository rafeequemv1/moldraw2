Aldol Condensation

Aldol condensation forms a carbon-carbon bond through enolate addition to a carbonyl compound, followed by dehydration to an alpha,beta-unsaturated aldehyde or ketone.

Common search intent: aldol condensation mechanism, examples, product prediction, and reaction scheme drawing.

Reaction Scheme

aldehyde or ketone + aldehyde or ketone -> alpha,beta-unsaturated carbonylBase or acid catalysis; heat often promotes dehydration.

Primary reaction SMILES: CC=O.CC=O>>C/C=C/C=O

Key Facts

Reaction type
Carbon-carbon bond formation; condensation
Typical reagents
NaOH, KOH, alkoxide base, acid catalyst, heat
Product class
Enal or enone

Two Common Examples

Acetaldehyde aldol condensation

Acetaldehyde can form crotonaldehyde after aldol addition and dehydration.

Acetone self-condensation

Acetone can form mesityl oxide under basic conditions after dehydration.
