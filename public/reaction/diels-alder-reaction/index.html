Diels-Alder Reaction

The Diels-Alder reaction is a concerted [4+2] cycloaddition between a conjugated diene and a dienophile, forming cyclohexene frameworks with predictable stereochemical outcomes.

Reaction Scheme

conjugated diene + dienophile -> cyclohexene derivativeThermal reaction; electron-rich dienes and electron-poor dienophiles are common.

Primary reaction SMILES: C=CC=C.C=CC=O>>O=CC1C=CCCC1

Key Facts

Reaction type
Concerted pericyclic cycloaddition
Typical partners
Diene plus alkene or alkyne dienophile
Product class
Cyclohexene or bicyclic ring system

Two Common Examples

Butadiene + ethene

Classic teaching example forming cyclohexene.

Cyclopentadiene + maleic anhydride

Common lab example showing endo selectivity.
