How CAS to Structure Lookup Works

CAS numbers are resolved through public PubChem synonym records. The tool then uses the matching PubChem CID to display a 2D structure image and metadata.

  • Verify the result for salts, mixtures, hydrates, and ambiguous registry records.
  • Use the SMILES value to open the compound in MolDraw or convert it to other identifiers.
