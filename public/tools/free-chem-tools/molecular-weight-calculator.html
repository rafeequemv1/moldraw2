How Molecular Weight Is Calculated

Molecular weight, also called molar mass or formula weight, is calculated by multiplying each element count by its atomic weight and summing the values. The result is reported in g/mol for one mole of formula units.

  • H2O: 2 hydrogens plus 1 oxygen.
  • Ca(OH)2: parentheses multiply oxygen and hydrogen by 2.
  • Al2(SO4)3: nested-style formula groups are expanded before summing.
