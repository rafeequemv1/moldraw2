About SMILES and Molecular Formula

SMILES describes molecular connectivity as a compact text string. A molecular formula summarizes the atom counts in the molecule, such as C9H8O4 for aspirin.

  • Use formula output for reports, exact mass checks, and composition calculations.
  • Use the molecular weight tool when you need g/mol from the formula.
